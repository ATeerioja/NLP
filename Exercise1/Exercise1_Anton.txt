PART A
The issue is dedicated to English documents writing. The work is aimed at analyzing linguistic peculiarities of 
drafting legal documents. The authors have noticed that while participating at Willem C. Vis annual 
International Commercial Arbitration moots in Vienna, their students show some difficulties in writing 
memoranda. Some years ago we were not teaching legal writing. Visiting San Diego University we paid 
attention to special courses taught there. Now that we organized special courses teaching legal writing, in 
this work the authors pay attention to business documents such as contracts, legal correspondence, claims, 
etc., considering that rights and duties must be validated by means of documents. In view of this, judicial 
documents should be drafted correctly. We truly believe that teaching documents writing now, at university, 
is a very important issue if we want to bring up comprehendible generation of lawyers. The work lays 
particular emphasis on the formal business language which is referred to as officialese and differs from other 
types of the English language, mostly due to specific characters of the functional usage that may be featured 
in classical terms of style, predetermination and main characteristics.
